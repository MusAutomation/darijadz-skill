# Credits

## Data used to validate spellings and word choices
DarijaDZ's examples and lexicon were **written by hand**. The datasets below were used to **check** them: to count how often a word or spelling appears in real Algerian speech and to cross-check meanings. No dataset text is redistributed in this repository. The frequency figures quoted in the reference files are derived statistics.

| Dataset | Licence | How it was used |
|---|---|---|
| [touati-kamel/algerian-darja-corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/touati-kamel/algerian-darja-corpus), Algerian Darija speech transcripts (~34M words) | CC BY 4.0 | word and bigram frequencies for spelling standards and attestation |
| [ayoubkirouane/Algerian-Darija](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ayoubkirouane/Algerian-Darija) | CC BY 4.0 | cross-checking vocabulary |
| [awras/algerian-darija-dictionary-v1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/awras/algerian-darija-dictionary-v1) | CC BY 4.0 | cross-checking word meanings |
| [algerian-nlp/DziriAlign](https://huggingface.co/datasets/algerian-nlp/DziriAlign) | MIT | cross-checking translations and register |
| [algerian-nlp/DziriEval](https://huggingface.co/datasets/algerian-nlp/DziriEval) | MIT | ideas for evaluation |

Thank you to the authors of these datasets for making Algerian Darija visible in AI.
