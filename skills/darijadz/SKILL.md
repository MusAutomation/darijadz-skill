# DarijaDZ: real Algerian Darija

Write like an Algerian talking to another Algerian: short spoken sentences with Algerian grammar and Algerian words. French or English slips in where people really use it. It is never فصحى, Moroccan, Tunisian or Egyptian, and never a word-for-word translation.

## 1. Pick the register first

| Situation | How it sounds | فصحى allowed | French / English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friends, family, chat | casual Darija | no | everyday words (normal, déjà, ok) |
| Customer DMs, customer service | polite Darija: مرحبا بيك، يعطيك الصحة، سمحلنا | no | trade words (stop desk, taille, promo) |
| Ads, product posts | Darija plus the standard ad formulas | only fixed formulas (الدفع عند الاستلام، جودة عالية) | yes |
| Video scripts, hooks, captions | punchy spoken Darija, one idea per line | no | yes, as people say it |
| Middle school and lycée (CEM, BEM, BAC) | Darija explanations | keep the **school terms in فصحى** (المعادلة، الخلية، الفاعل) | only in language classes |
| University | Darija glue | rarely | **science terms in French, Latin letters** (la dérivée، le TD، le mémoire) |
| Business, freelance, agency | polite and direct Darija | no | business words (devis, deadline, meeting, client) |
| Official letters, contracts, legal | Darija is wrong here | say so and offer MSA or French | |

If the audience is unclear, use friendly, polite Darija that works for everyone from a teenager to a parent. `references/registers.md` shows the same message written in five registers.

## 2. Pick the script

- **Default is mixed.** Darija goes in Arabic letters and French or English words go in Latin letters: `راني نخدم على le projet، نبعثلك le lien دروك`.
- Common loanwords that became Darija are written in **Arabic letters**: كوموند، نورمال، ديجا، ميساج، لاباج، النيميرو، البورطابل، الطوموبيل، الكونيكسيون، الفليكسي، أبوني، ليفرور. The full list is in `references/writing-system.md`.
- Use **Arabic letters only** when the user asks for it (some teachers and parents prefer it), and **Arabizi** (`wach rak, rani mli7`) when the user asks or writes to you in Arabizi. **Mirror the user's script.**
- Write prices as digits with **دج / DA**. One **مليون** is 10,000 DA (it counts centimes). See `references/numbers-money-time.md`.

## 3. Ten rules of Algerian grammar

1. **Think in Darija and restructure.** Don't translate word by word: `على الرغم من التعب، أكمل العمل` becomes `حتى لوكان عيان، كمّل الخدمة`.
2. **Right now: راني / راك / راكي / راه / راهي / رانا / راكم / راهم + verb or adjective.** `راني نحوس، راهي مريضة`. The negative forms are مانيش، ماكش، ماهوش، ماهيش، ماناش، ماكمش، ماهمش: `مانيش فاهم`.
3. **Future with راح + present**: `راح نبعثلك`. Never use سوف or سـ. The west also says غادي. Use **باش** for "in order to": `باش نفهم`. <!-- lint:ignore -->
4. **Negation.** Verbs take **ما…ش**: `ما نعرفش، ما كانش`. Nouns and adjectives take **ماشي**: `ماشي غالية`. For nothing, nobody and never use والو، حتى واحد and عمري ما. Imperatives become `ما تقلقش`.
5. **Possession with تاع**: `الطوموبيل تاع خويا، الكوموند تاعك`, or a suffix: `داري، خويا`. Never use ديال, which is Moroccan. <!-- lint:ignore -->
6. **Questions**: واش (what / yes-no), علاش, كيفاش, وين, منين, وقتاش, شحال, شكون, واش من (which). Never use ماذا, لماذا, متى, أين, هل or كيف حالك. <!-- lint:ignore -->
7. **Verb forms**. In the present: نـ for I, تـ for you and she, يـ for he. Plurals add **و**: `نديرو، تديرو، يديرو`. In the past: `درت، درتي، دار، دارت، درنا، درتو، داروا`. Feminine "you" adds ي: `تديري، درتي`.
8. **Glue the pronouns on**: `قالّي، نبعثلك، عيطلي، شفتو، جيبهالي، نبدلوهالك`.
9. **Algerian connectors**: بصح، على خاطر، باش، بلي، كي، لوكان، إذا، حتى، غير، برك، كيما، هكا، زعما، تسمى، يعني، مالا، المهم، بالاك.
10. **Algerian words**, checked against real speech: دروك، بزاف، شوية، كاين، ما كانش، كاع، كامل، مليح، شاب، هايل، حاجة، صوالح، يهضر، يحوس، يخمم، يسقسي، يعيط، يستنى، يخلّص، يبعث، الدراهم، السومة، البورطابل، الطوموبيل، الدار، الخدمة، السمانة، غدوة، البارح.

The full grammar with conjugation tables is in `references/grammar.md`, and 166 verbs are in `references/verbs.md`.

## 6. Honesty rules for sellers and creators

- Never invent reviews, customer numbers, "original" claims, warranties or fake scarcity. If the user gives the facts, use them; if not, leave a placeholder like `[السومة]` or `[شحال بقا في الستوك]`.
- Don't promise health, beauty or money results. Write `النتيجة تختلف من واحد لواحد` instead.
- An ad is labelled as an ad when a creator is paid: `هادا إشهار`.

## 7. Check before you send

- [ ] No word from the table in section 4 is left.
- [ ] The register matches the audience from section 1, and school terms or French terms are where they belong.
- [ ] The script follows section 2 and mirrors the user.
- [ ] Verbs use راني / راح / ما…ش and gender agrees (نتي تديري، هي دارت).
- [ ] Prices are digits with دج / DA, and مليون is converted correctly.
- [ ] Read it in your head with an Algerian voice. If it sounds like dubbed TV or a translation, rewrite it shorter.
- If you can run code, lint the text: `python3 scripts/darija_lint.py draft.txt`. It flags MSA, Moroccan, Tunisian and Egyptian words and suggests the Algerian one.

## 8. Regions and corrections

Darija changes from Oran to Algiers to Constantine to the south. Default to the widely understood central and urban Darija used above. When the user uses a regional word (وشنو، غادي، علاه، قداه، نتي for both genders…) or corrects you, **adopt their word for the rest of the conversation** without arguing. Their Darija is the reference. <!-- lint:ignore -->

## 9. Output

- Answer with the Darija directly. Add an English or French line only if the user isn't Algerian or asks for it.
- For hooks, captions and ad lines, give 3 to 5 options with different angles.
- Keep the user's emojis, numbers, product names and links exactly as given.
