# Code-switching: French and English inside Darija

Algerians switch to French, and more and more to English, inside the same sentence. Done right, it sounds native. Done wrong, it sounds like a French sentence with Arabic stickers on it.

## The golden rule
**Darija carries the grammar and French or English brings the nouns and terms.** Verbs, pronouns, negation, question words and connectors stay Darija.

<!-- lint:off -->
| ✗ sounds wrong | ✓ sounds Algerian |
|---|---|
| Je vais t'envoyer le lien دروك | نبعثلك le lien دروك |
| Il faut confirmer la commande باش نبعثو | لازم نكونفيرميو الكوموند باش نبعثو |
| The client ما ردّش because he was busy | الكليان ما ردّش خاطر كان مشغول |
<!-- lint:on -->

## Five patterns people really use

1. **A French noun in a Darija sentence, with its French article.** `راني نحضّر le mémoire`, `جاتني la commande`, `الكونيكسيون ثقيلة`.
2. **French connectors and fillers.** donc, mais, parce que, normalement, franchement, déjà, en tout cas, voilà, bon, c'est bon, bref. Example: `franchement، فكرة مليحة`, `normalement توصل غدوة`.
3. **French verbs with Darija grammar, written in Arabic letters.** نكوموندي، يكونفيرمي، نشارجي، بلوكاني، بارطاجيو، تيليشارجيت، نفيكسيو، ما ريبونديليش. They conjugate like Darija verbs (see `grammar.md`).
4. **Short fixed French phrases.** `c'est pas grave`, `ça marche`, `pas de problème`, `bon courage`, `à demain`. Don't build whole French sentences.
5. **English tech and creator words.** AI, prompt, agent, automation, workflow, story, reel, live, follow, DM, CTA, hook, content, feedback, deadline. Example: `الـ agent يجاوب في الـ DM وحدو`.

## How much to mix (by audience)

| Audience | Mix |
|---|---|
| Young urban, creators, tech, e-commerce | a lot: 1 to 3 French or English words per sentence is normal |
| General public, parents, customers | light: everyday loans (نورمال، ديجا، كوموند) plus the trade words |
| Middle school, lycée | almost none in the explanations; subject terms in فصحى |
| University sciences | French terms in Latin letters, with a Darija explanation |
| Older people, rural audiences | very light, Darija first |

## Translating from French or English
Don't map words one by one. Ask yourself how an Algerian would say this idea out loud.

<!-- lint:off -->
| source | literal (bad) | Darija (good) |
|---|---|---|
| Je suis en train de travailler. | أنا في طريقة الخدمة | راني نخدم |
| Il faut que tu viennes. | يجب أن تأتي | لازم تجي |
| I'm looking forward to it. | أنا نتطلع ليها | راني نستنى فيها بزاف / راني حاب نشوفها |
| It's not worth it. | ماشي يستاهل | ما يسواش |
| Don't hesitate to contact us. | ما تترددش باش تتصل بينا | ابعثلنا بلا حشمة / عيطلنا وقت ما تحب |
| Free delivery from two items. | توصيل مجاني من زوج عناصر | التوصيل بلاش كي تشري زوج |
| We're sorry for the inconvenience. | نعتذر على الإزعاج | سمحلنا على هاد الشي |
<!-- lint:on -->

## Gender and articles of French words
- Keep the French gender: `la commande تاعك وصلت`, `le colis وصل`, `la connexion ثقيلة`.
- Use the Arabic article with a space for English words: `الـ agent`, `الـ automation`, `الـ CTA`.
- For a French plural, use the French article plus a Darija possessive: `les clients تاعي`, `les notes تاوعي`.

## Numbers and time in mixed speech
People often say numbers and times in French (`deux mille`, `à neuf heures`), but in writing use digits: `2000 دج`, `على 9`.
