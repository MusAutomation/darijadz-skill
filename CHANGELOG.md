# Changelog

## v2.0.0 (2026-11)
- 1,659 hand-written examples in 20 domains: e-commerce DMs, flows and ads; customer service; content creators; middle school, lycée and university; business; tech and AI; feelings; religion and culture; services and admin; فصحى → Darija; French and English → Darija; three scripts; grammar; proverbs; dialogues.
- A 689-entry lexicon (523 words + 166 verbs) with Arabizi, French, the فصحى to avoid, and notes. Spellings were checked against word frequencies from a 34M-word corpus of Algerian speech.
- New references: writing system, full grammar, verbs, code-switching, registers, numbers / money / time, regions, pitfalls, plus domain playbooks for e-commerce, content creators, education and business.
- `darija_lint.py`: flags MSA, Moroccan, Tunisian, Egyptian, Levantine and Gulf words, with Algerian suggestions.
- Evals: 20 prompts plus a scoring rubric.
- A universal prompt for ChatGPT, Gemini and other AIs.
- Fixes from v1: رانا (not راحنا) for "we are"; لازم instead of خاصك; هضر over هدر; ڨ (not ڤ) for the g sound; غادي marked as western Algerian, not Moroccan; يعيشك removed (Tunisian); 69 wilayas from 2027.
